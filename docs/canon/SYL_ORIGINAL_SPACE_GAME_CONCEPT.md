# Space You Land — Original Space Game Concept

This is the original concept text Jaron K. Bragg supplied on 2026-08-15 as the
inspiration for Space You Land. It is preserved as design origin and context.
The locked v3.0.1 Bible and accepted amendments in `CANON.md` govern if wording
here conflicts with later canon.

## Core Concept

- **Setting:** The game is set in space, featuring various factions, both
  AI-controlled and player-created, each with distinct political, economic, and
  strategic goals.
- **Genre:** Cross-platform RPG shooter with free roam capabilities.
- **Gameplay:** Players explore an open-world universe, controlling spaceships,
  interacting with factions, and engaging in complex political, economic, and
  strategic systems. The game blends exploration, combat, and diplomacy in a
  vast space environment.

## Key Gameplay Features

### Spaceships and Factions

- **Spaceships:** Players will control customizable spaceships, each with its
  own capabilities for travel, combat, and trade. Ships can be upgraded with
  advanced technologies and unique features.
- **Factions:**
  - **Game-Controlled Factions (PvE):** Established factions in the game will
    have their own political structures, territories, and economies. Players
    can engage with these factions in safer, PvE zones.
  - **Player-Created Factions (PvP):** Players can form their own factions,
    allowing for PvP gameplay in specific zones. These factions are subject to
    rivalries, territorial disputes, and open warfare.

### Political, Economic, and Strategic Systems

- **Political System:** Players can align with, betray, or manipulate factions.
  Factions will react to player actions, leading to alliances, wars, or trade
  agreements.
- **Economic System:**
  - **Dynamic Player-Driven Economy:** Prices of resources fluctuate based on
    supply and demand. Players can engage in trade, resource gathering, and
    manufacturing, impacting the larger economy.
  - **Station Economy:** Players can own and manage space stations as trade
    hubs, charging tariffs, setting taxes, and providing services such as ship
    repairs and refueling. Stations can be customized with defenses,
    marketplaces, and manufacturing capabilities.
  - **Trade Routes:** Players can create and manage trade routes, protecting
    them from pirates or rival factions. Successful trade boosts a faction's
    economy, while attacks on trade convoys can destabilize it.
  - **Resource Management:** Players can mine and harvest resources from
    planets, asteroids, or other celestial bodies, feeding the economy and
    their fleet's progression.
- **Strategic System:**
  - **Faction Warfare:** Both game-controlled and player-created factions can
    wage war over territories, resources, and political control. Territory
    control affects resources, trade routes, and strategic advantages.
  - **Alliance Building:** Players can forge alliances with factions, pooling
    fleets and resources for large-scale battles. Diplomacy plays a key role in
    maintaining these alliances.
  - **Fleet Management:** Players can control entire fleets, balancing offense,
    defense, and support ships. Fleet composition and tactics will determine
    success in large-scale battles.
  - **Territorial Control:** Factions can expand by conquering planets,
    stations, and key resources, gaining dominance over sectors of the galaxy.

### RPG Elements

- **Character and Ship Customization:** Players can develop their characters
  and upgrade their spaceships with better weapons, shields, and engines. Skill
  trees for combat, diplomacy, engineering, and navigation will allow players
  to specialize.
- **Faction Influence:** Players' actions shape the game world, influencing
  political alliances, trade agreements, and military conflicts. Each decision
  could shift the balance of power between factions.
- **Morale System:** Crew morale will affect ship efficiency, with poor morale
  potentially leading to sabotage or mutiny. Players must manage crew happiness
  through victories, rewards, and living conditions.

### Dynamic Universe

- **Living Universe:** The game features a living universe where planets and
  factions evolve based on player actions. Factions will colonize planets,
  build new stations, or face extinction, making the game world constantly
  changing.
- **Procedurally Generated Missions:** Players can take on procedurally
  generated missions based on the needs of factions, such as resource
  gathering, combat, or espionage.
- **Exploration Events:** Players may encounter alien species, ancient
  civilizations, and rare technologies as they explore deep space.

## Advanced Features

### Faction Warfare and Politics

- **Player-Created Factions:** Players can establish their own factions,
  managing political systems, trade, and military might. These factions are
  fully PvP, with risks of internal and external conflict.
- **Faction Reputation and Diplomacy:** Players can influence faction politics,
  making or breaking alliances, and shaping the political landscape of the
  galaxy. Espionage, bribery, and sabotage can be used to gain an advantage.
- **Galactic Council:** Major game-controlled factions form a Galactic Council,
  where players with high enough influence can participate. Decisions like
  trade laws, wars, and sanctions affect the entire galaxy. Players can sway
  these decisions through diplomacy or coercion.

### Economy and Trade

- **Dynamic Player-Driven Economy:** The economy evolves as players trade, mine
  resources, and manufacture goods. Supply and demand impact prices, with
  players able to corner markets, manipulate resources, or engage in economic
  warfare.
- **Black Market:** Players can participate in the black market, dealing in
  illegal goods, alien tech, or stolen resources. The black market offers high
  rewards but comes with the risk of bounty hunters and law enforcement
  factions targeting smugglers.

### Stations and Fleets

- **Space Station Customization:** Players can build and own space stations,
  customizing them with defenses, trade hubs, and research facilities. Stations
  serve as key hubs for factions, controlling strategic points in the galaxy.
- **Fleet Progression:** Players can build and upgrade fleets, progressing from
  small fighter squadrons to massive capital ship fleets. Special elite units
  and skilled commanders add bonuses to fleet performance.
- **Fleet Command:** Players can control their fleet's movement, tactics, and
  battle strategies, allowing for dynamic combat scenarios. They can also
  deploy scouting.
